# How AI is used on Volley!

AI is a tool the maintainer uses. This is a notice of where.

**AI is used for:**

- Writing and reviewing code, tests, and configuration.
- Drafting technical documentation.
- Root-cause analysis and CI workflow work.

**AI is not used for:**

- The world, the story, the characters, or any of the game's prose.
- Art, music, or sound. No generative-AI assets ship in the game.
- Marketing, devlogs, or replies in community spaces.
- Merge decisions. Pull requests merge when the maintainer merges them by hand.

Every commit is signed off by the maintainer under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org). What tool produced a change does not change who is responsible for it.
