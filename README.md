# Volley!

An idle pong game that lives on your desktop. A small window, always there, gently bouncing away while you work. The main character has a dream: beat the world volley record. You train, buy items, recruit partners, and inch toward that number one bounce at a time.

Built in [Godot](https://godotengine.org) and developed in the open.

## How AI is used

Volley! is developed with AI assistance (Claude Code) folded into the open-development practice. The maintainer signs off every commit, AI does not author fiction or art, and merge decisions stay human. See [AI.md](AI.md) for the full picture, and [The Case for Open Development](docs/research/the-case-for-open-development.md) for why building in the open is the bigger move underneath this one.
